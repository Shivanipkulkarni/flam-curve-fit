Why the transformation matters

After undoing the translation and rotation, the first coordinate becomes u = t and the second becomes v(t) = eM|t| sin(0.3t). The apparent complexity is therefore mostly a coordinate transformation.

Why point order does not matter

The shuffled rows do not need to be reconstructed into a sequence. For a candidate (θ, M, X), each point gives its own t directly. The optimization only searches over the three global parameters.
