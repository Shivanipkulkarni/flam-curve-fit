01 — What's actually hidden here

Strip out θ and the translation, and the equation is just v(t) = e^(M|t|)·sin(0.3t) — an amplitude-modulated sine wave, the most ordinary signal there is. Everything else is a 2D rotation and a shift dressed on top to obscure it.

02 — Why order didn't matter

The 1500 points arrived shuffled, not traced in order. Rotating each point backward by −θ recovers its own t directly — u = (x−X)cosθ + (y−42)sinθ — so no per-point unknowns were needed, just one global (θ, M, X) fit.
